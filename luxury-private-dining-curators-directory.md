## Gaps & notes

- **Cebu** has no established independent supper-club scene visible on the open web (unlike Manila). Closest high-fit partners: Sialo (private hire), The PHat Chef (Cebu-serving), Triphappy (yacht dinners). A Facebook-only "Chef's Kitchen" (bespoke home fine dining, Cebu) surfaced via the Kaon Nasad food page but has no discoverable page — worth scouting locally.
- **Bacolod/Iloilo** turned up no independent private-dining curators beyond Take a Chef's local rosters.
- Several Manila supper clubs (Lasa, Pasticci, Omotenashi, Idalia) book **exclusively via Instagram DM** — no email/phone published anywhere.
- All contact details are publicly published info from the businesses' own pages/listings; verify each on the cited source before first outreach, as snippets can lag behind current info.
