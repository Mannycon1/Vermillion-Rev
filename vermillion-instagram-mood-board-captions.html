Voice Notes Applied Throughout

  • Informed and insightful — captions lead with real local knowledge (Dinagyang, Hablon, batchoy, Pancit Molo, cityhood facts) instead of generic praise
  • Clear and concise — short sentences, bullets for lists, no fluff or clichés ("world-class," "dream stay," and similar are avoided)
  • Reliably approachable — readers are "you," the brand is "we"; friendly professional, never overly formal or overly enthusiastic
  • Understated confidence — bold and direct where it earns it ("Your pace, your call."), never boastful
  • Style guide — 12-hour time with no space (4PM, 6 to 10AM), "midnight" spelled out, small numbers written out, metric measurements, US spelling
  • Strategy topics translated — internal items (challenges, personas, brainstorm coinages) are reframed as guest-facing angles so every topic can live on Instagram
