## Voice Notes Applied Throughout

- **Informed and insightful** — captions lead with real local knowledge (Dinagyang,
  Hablon, batchoy, cityhood facts) instead of generic praise
- **Clear and concise** — short sentences, bullets for amenity lists, no fluff or
  clichés ("world-class," "dream stay," and similar are avoided)
- **Reliably approachable** — readers are "you," the brand is "we"; friendly
  professional, never overly formal or overly enthusiastic (no exclamation-mark stacking)
- **Understated confidence** — bold and direct where it earns it ("Your pace, your
  call."), never boastful
- **Style guide** — 12-hour time with no space (4PM, 6 to 10AM, spelled-out
  "midnight"), small numbers written out ("three generations"), metric measurements,
  US spelling
